# Privacy notice for the reviewed native candidate

Messages and ordinary private media/history stay in the app's encrypted phone storage and use direct encrypted peer transports. Cloudflare/FCM/Google sign-in services process coordination, consent/block/push/authentication metadata. Their use is distinct from carrying chat/file/media payloads. Optional presence/notification previews and device Contacts export follow user settings. Contacts saved through the phone can be readable/synced through the selected phone account; Google encrypted contact backup is not implemented.

Quick Share saves completed accepted original files OUTSIDE the private vault to Downloads/Cute Chat. Other apps/backups can access such copies, and they cannot be recalled. App-private temporary data may exist for permitted viewers/updates; do not claim zero device traces or universal screenshot prevention.

Update checks and downloads contact public GitHub/CDN, which may see IP/request metadata. No peer/contact ID, Google token, message or private cryptographic key is included in update requests. Download and installation are user actions; Android installation permission is asked only when needed. This APK mechanism is not a silent installer or Play Store update implementation. Device/app force-stop, no power/network and Android/OEM rules remain limits.
